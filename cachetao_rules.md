# Cachetão - Official Game Rules

**Cachetão** (a popular variant of Cacheta/Pife) is a fast-paced card game focused on forming valid combinations (melds) to empty your hand.

## 1. The Deck and Players
- **Players:** 2 to 9 players.
- **Deck:** Two standard 52-card decks (104 cards total). Jokers are usually removed, but this depends on the specific variant.
- **Hand Size:** 9 cards are dealt to each player. 

## 2. The Objective
The goal is to be the first player to form valid combinations (melds) with all 9 cards in your hand (or 10 cards, if you "bate" with the card drawn from the deck). 

When a player achieves this, they declare **"Bate"** (Knock) and win the round.

## 3. Valid Combinations (Melds)
To win, your cards must be arranged into valid groups of at least 3 cards. 

**A. Trinca (Set)**
- 3 or 4 cards of the **same rank**. 
- A Trinca MUST contain at least **3 unique suits**.
- *Example (3 cards):* 7 of Hearts, 7 of Spades, 7 of Clubs (must be all different suits).
- *Example (4 cards):* 9 of Clubs, 9 of Clubs, 9 of Diamonds, 9 of Spades (a 4-card Trinca typically contains a double card like two 9s of Clubs, but still has 3 unique suits overall).

**B. Sequência (Straight / Run)**
- 3 or 4 cards of the **same suit** in **numerical sequence**.
- The deck ranks range from **Ace (1) to King (13)**.
- *Example (3 cards):* 4, 5, and 6 of Spades.
- *Example (4 cards):* 7, 8, 9, and 10 of Hearts (a 4-card sequence is perfectly valid for a 10-card Pife).
- *Note:* The sequence A-2-3 is valid, as is J-Q-K or Q-K-A (Ace can be high or low). However, wrapping around like K-A-2 is usually *not* valid.

## 4. The Turn Sequence
The game proceeds clockwise. On your turn, you must:

1. **Draw a Card:** 
   - You may draw the top, face-down card from the **Deck**.
   - OR you may pick up the top, face-up card from the **Discard Pile** (some variants only allow this if that specific card completes a meld in your hand).
2. **Discard a Card:**
   - You must choose one card from your hand and place it face-up on the Discard Pile.
   - Your turn is now over.

## 5. The "Coringa" (Wildcard)
At the start of the round, after hands are dealt, the dealer flips the top card of the deck face-up on the table.
- The **next card in numerical sequence** (of the same suit, or sometimes of any suit depending on house rules) becomes the **Coringa** (Wildcard) for that round.
- *Example:* If the flipped card is a 5 of Hearts, all 6s (or specifically the 6 of Hearts and its matching color) become Wildcards. 
- A Wildcard can substitute for *any* card to complete a Trinca or Sequência.

## 6. Winning the Game (Bater)
- **Normal Bate:** You have 9 cards perfectly melded, and you discard your 10th drawn card.
- **Pife:** Sometimes a player is dealt 9 perfectly melded cards right off the bat, or they draw their 10th card and all 10 can be melded without needing to discard.

## 7. Scoring (Points System)
Players start with **10 points** each. 
- **Normal Win (Bater with 9 cards):** The winner takes no damage. All other players lose **1 point**.
- **Pife Win (Bater with 10 cards without discarding):** The winner takes no damage. All other players lose **2 points** (because hitting a 10-card hand is significantly harder).
- A player is eliminated when their score reaches 0. The last player standing wins the game.
